# PageHeading

The claim that opens each view: a short full sentence in `page-title`, then a `lead` explaining what the data replaces.

- Provide: a title of 3–7 words ending with a period ("Le prix juste, déjà calculé.", "Demandez comme à un métreur.") and one or two lead sentences in ink-muted, max 780px.
- The lead names the before (tableur, métreur, Excel) and the after. Concrete, no superlatives.
- 26px below it before the KPI row.
